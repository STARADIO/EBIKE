# S700SE

**Moped Economy** — 19 inch · 52 V · 1500 W rear hub

---

## Why this one

The value end of the moped line. 1500 W of rear hub drive, a 52 V 25 Ah pack, and
the running gear to match — 203 mm hydraulic discs and dual 220 mm rear shocks,
which is more than you would expect at this price.

It is the model we spec when the priority is getting home reliably rather than
maximising anything.

---

## On top speed

We do not publish a speed figure for this model yet.

Its hardware is capable of more than the legal limit in most markets, and the
factory limit for each destination is still being confirmed. Rather than print a
number we cannot stand behind, **ask us for the configured limit for your market,
or book a test ride.**

## Highlights

- **1500 W rear hub motor**
- **52 V 25 Ah** battery — long-range pack for the class
- **Dual hydraulic disc brakes** — 203 mm rotors
- **Dual rear shocks** — 220 mm, 1200 lb
- **Aluminium hydraulic fork** — 110 mm
- **Colour LCD display** with status readout
