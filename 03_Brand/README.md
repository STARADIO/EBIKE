# Brand

## What we build

Seven e-bikes, from a 25 km/h commuter to a 3000 W moped. What connects them is not
a price point or a styling language — it is that we would rather cut a specification
than publish a number we cannot stand behind.

That is why this repo exists. A marketing repo is the easiest place in a company to
start overstating things, because everything in it is designed to make the product
sound better. We decided it would be more useful as a shop window if it were also
the most technically honest document we publish.

---

## Three things we actually commit to

**1. Speed limits we can defend.**
Every bike ships limited to the legal ceiling for its destination market. Not "up
to", not "in some configurations" — set in the controller, locked before shipping.
Where a model's limit is not yet confirmed, we publish no figure and invite you to
test-ride. We would rather leave a cell blank than fill it with a datasheet number.

**2. Certification stated honestly.**
We hold UL 2271 for batteries, UL 1004 materials for motors, ISO 13849 for
controllers, CE + UL for chargers, CE + RoHS for displays. Those are real and they
are component-scope. We do not describe a whole bike as certified on the strength
of its battery. Whole-vehicle certification for the U.S. is in progress, and until
it lands the wording stays careful.

**3. Specifications that match the bike.**
Every published spec is one we have verified against a built unit. Where a figure is
still being measured, it is absent from this repo — not present with a hedge in the
footnotes.

---

## How we write

Plain sentences. No "revolutionary", no "unleash", no "elevate your commute". If a
bike has dual motors, we say dual motors and give the wattage. The reader is
probably an engineer or a shop owner — they can tell.

## Assets

Logo and brand template files live in [`05_Assets/`](../05_Assets/). Photography
follows a fixed angle convention across every model so that the same asset can be
used on our own storefront and on third-party marketplaces without re-shooting.
